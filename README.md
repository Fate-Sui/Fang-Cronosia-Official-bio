<h1>Part 01: The Last One</h1>
<p>The scene opens in cold, snowy weather. Marshall, who is only 6 years old, is with her newborn baby brother, Carlos, whom she is pulling along in a cart. She is starving, exhausted, and desperate, until finally… Marshall collapses.</p>

<p>The story then flashes back to three weeks prior in a remote village, where Marshall's mother is heavily pregnant and about to give birth. Marshall is thrilled at the thought of becoming an older sister, especially after hearing that her sibling is a boy. After a long wait, the sound of a newborn baby crying fills the air—Marshall's little brother is born and given the name Carlos D. Roman.

Suddenly, Marshall senses something strange happening to her body and soul. She screams, unleashing a massive burst of energy that illuminates the entire village. When the light fades, Marshall wakes up—only to be horrified by what she sees. HER FATHER AND MOTHER HAVE BEEN REDUCED TO SKELETONS. Meanwhile, Carlos is still crying in his mother’s arms, which are now nothing more than bare bone. Marshall runs outside to call for help, but every patient, visitor, doctor, and nurse in the clinic has also been turned into skeletons.</p>

<p>Catching her reflection in a mirror, Marshall gasps: she now has horns and terrifying, demon-like eyes. She struggles to process what is happening, desperately hoping it's all just a nightmare. But Carlos's worsening cries are a harsh reminder that this nightmare is real.

In the end, Marshall gathers Carlos and leaves immediately. She soon realizes that the tragedy isn't confined to the health center—everyone in the village has met the same fate. She does her absolute best to take care of Carlos, enduring long nights of listening to his crying before he finally goes quiet.</p>

<p>After several days, Marshall runs out of food and supplies. Knowing there is no hope left in the village, she decides to venture out into the unknown, carrying Carlos with her. Days pass into weeks, marking the lowest point of Marshall's life. Forced to beg in markets and towns, she often ends up sleeping on the pavement outside storefronts. Whenever she reaches out for help, people reject and drive her away. They are terrified of her monstrous horns and demonic eyes. At times, she is even chased off by local sheriffs and angry mobs. Left with no other choice, Marshall turns to stealing—taking food and infant formula from shops just to keep Carlos alive.</p>

<p>Eventually, snow begins to fall. Marshall wraps herself in a jacket and swaddles Carlos in a thick blanket. But the freezing temperatures are unbearable, even with her coat. Marshall collapses into the snow, losing all hope—hope in humanity, and hope for herself and Carlos.

When Marshall opens her eyes again, she finds herself in a warm room softly lit by a lantern, lying on a comfortable, plush mattress. At first, she thinks she is dreaming. Then, a woman dressed as a nun enters, carrying a bowl of hot soup. The nun introduces herself as Syrena. Nearby, Carlos is fast asleep in a crib. Marshall is stunned—she has never seen Carlos sleep so peacefully.

Even more surprising is that Syrena seems completely unfazed by Marshall's demonic appearance. Before Marshall can ask about it, she notices that her horns are gone and her eyes have returned to normal. Syrena gently reassures her, saying she wasn't afraid of Marshall even when she brought her in with the horns and eyes—meaning the demonic form vanished on its own when Marshall was brought into Syrena's home.

From that day on, Syrena takes Marshall and Carlos under her wing, caring for them without an ounce of hatred or prejudice. For the first time in a long while, Marshall gets to experience a sense of home again, confident that Carlos will grow up safe and loved.</p>

<p>Years pass. Marshall is now 12 years old, and Carlos is 6. They continue to live happily with Syrena, who has become like a mother to them. Marshall's demonic form has never returned, though she still secretly wonders what truly happened to her six years ago.</p>

<h1>Part 02: Darkness Within the Light</h1>
<p>One day, Syrena asks Marshall to go to the market to buy some spices and groceries. Marshall hurries off without delay, knowing the walk is quite far since their home sits isolated in the middle of rice fields. Meanwhile, Carlos is out on the field playing ball with the other kids, missing his chance to accompany her. Because Marshall still carries a bad reputation at the market from years ago—when people viewed her as a demon—she wears a hood to conceal her face. After gathering all the items on Syrena’s list, she makes her way back home.</p>

<p>Upon arriving, Marshall spots several sheriff cruisers parked outside. Stepping inside, she is shocked to find Syrena restrained by two sheriffs. Demanding an explanation, Marshall is told that Syrena is actually a drug lord. Stunned, Marshall vehemently refuses to believe it, convinced Syrena has been framed. But then, one of the officers holds up the evidence Syrena had been hiding all along: a thick liquid containing controlled substances. Speechless and faced with physical proof, Marshall can only turn to Syrena for answers.</p>

<p>Syrena looks at her and whispers, <i>"It's true, Marshall... I'm so sorry."</i></p>
<p>Marshall freezes, completely at a loss for words or actions. She can no longer defend Syrena.

At that moment, one of the sheriffs recognizes Marshall's face, recalling reports of a young blonde girl at the market with demonic features. Realizing Marshall has spent the last six years hiding out with a drug kingpin, they decide to arrest her as well. Marshall is paralyzed, but Syrena suddenly throws herself forward to restrain the officer trying to grab her, giving Marshall a chance to pull away.

As the situation rapidly escalates out of control, one of the officers draws his revolver and aims directly at Marshall. Realizing the danger instantly, Syrena leaps in front of Marshall to shield her from the oncoming bullet.</p>

<p><strong>BANG!!</strong></p>
<p>Syrena wraps her arms around Marshall to protect her, taking the bullet straight to the heart. Marshall’s eyes widen in horror as she feels Syrena’s grip go limp. Yet, Syrena smiles. Marshall cradles Syrena's head gently in her lap as Syrena whispers her final words:</p>
<p><i>"Happy birthday, Marshall... For the last time, I'm sorry..."</i></p>
<p>Syrena closes her eyes and takes her last breath right there in Marshall’s arms.

Marshall stares past the body—not at the officers, but toward the birthday decorations Syrena had secretly set up as a surprise while Marshall was at the market. Balloons and a banner reading "Happy 13th Birthday, Marshall" hang quietly in the room. In an instant, the peaceful, quiet world Marshall had known is shattered once again.

Something massive and dark surges and boils inside her. She grunts, gritting her teeth as the surrounding air grows heavy and suffocating. Setting Syrena gently onto the floor, Marshall rises. Her eyes darken, her pupils shifting to a fierce magenta, glaring at the sheriffs like an apex predator about to tear its prey apart.</p>

<p>One of the sheriffs fires a shot, but Marshall catches the bullet mid-air and crushes it with her bare hands, her fingers tipping into razor-sharp claws. Terrified, the sheriffs unleash a hail of gunfire until their chambers are empty, but Marshall effortlessly snatches every bullet out of the air before crushing them to dust. Cornered with nowhere to run, the sheriffs freeze.</p>
<p>Then, <strong>MARSHALL POUNCES</strong>.</p>
<p><strong><i>"AHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!"</strong></p>

<p>By dusk, Carlos finally heads home after playing too long with his friends. Anxious at the sight of the sheriff cars outside, he rushes indoors—only to be greeted by a gruesome, bloody scene. Syrena's lifeless body lies near the two sheriffs, whose remains are barely recognizable.

Standing among them is Marshall. Her cold, demonic eyes turn to look at Carlos. A shudder runs down Carlos's spine, sheer terror gripping him as he stares at his sister.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Marshall blurs forward, wrapping Carlos in a desperate, tight embrace, smearing blood from her face and hands onto him. Marshall sobs uncontrollably before breaking into hysterical tears. Carlos's terror turns to overwhelming confusion as his sister cries violently against him.</p>
<p>Marshall squeezes him tighter and whispers:</p>
<p><i>"Carlos... There is no truth left for us in this life... From now on... we go through everything together. JUST THE TWO OF US. And I won't lose you. No... I refuse to lose you."</i></p>
