<h1>Episode 01: The Last One</h1>
<p>The scene opens in cold, snowy weather. Marshall, who is only 6 years old, is with her newborn baby brother, Carlos, whom she is pulling along in a cart. She is starving, exhausted, and desperate, until finally… Marshall collapses.</p>

<p>The story then flashes back to three weeks prior in a remote village, where Marshall's mother is heavily pregnant and about to give birth. Marshall is thrilled at the thought of becoming an older sister, especially after hearing that her sibling is a boy. After a long wait, the sound of a newborn baby crying fills the air—Marshall's little brother is born and given the name Carlos D. Roman.

Suddenly, Marshall senses something strange happening to her body and soul. She screams, unleashing a massive burst of energy that illuminates the entire village. When the light fades, Marshall wakes up—only to be horrified by what she sees. HER FATHER AND MOTHER HAVE BEEN REDUCED TO SKELETONS. Meanwhile, Carlos is still crying in his mother’s arms, which are now nothing more than bare bone. Marshall runs outside to call for help, but every patient, visitor, doctor, and nurse in the clinic has also been turned into skeletons.</p>

<p>Catching her reflection in a mirror, Marshall gasps: she now has horns and terrifying, demon-like eyes. She struggles to process what is happening, desperately hoping it's all just a nightmare. But Carlos's worsening cries are a harsh reminder that this nightmare is real.

In the end, Marshall gathers Carlos and leaves immediately. She soon realizes that the tragedy isn't confined to the health center—everyone in the village has met the same fate. She does her absolute best to take care of Carlos, enduring long nights of listening to his crying before he finally goes quiet.</p>

<p>After several days, Marshall runs out of food and supplies. Knowing there is no hope left in the village, she decides to venture out into the unknown, carrying Carlos with her. Days pass into weeks, marking the lowest point of Marshall's life. Forced to beg in markets and towns, she often ends up sleeping on the pavement outside storefronts. Whenever she reaches out for help, people reject and drive her away. They are terrified of her monstrous horns and demonic eyes. At times, she is even chased off by local sheriffs and angry mobs. Left with no other choice, Marshall turns to stealing—taking food and infant formula from shops just to keep Carlos alive.</p>

<p>Eventually, snow begins to fall. Marshall wraps herself in a jacket and swaddles Carlos in a thick blanket. But the freezing temperatures are unbearable, even with her coat. Marshall collapses into the snow, losing all hope—hope in humanity, and hope for herself and Carlos.

When Marshall opens her eyes again, she finds herself in a warm room softly lit by a lantern, lying on a comfortable, plush mattress. At first, she thinks she is dreaming. Then, a woman dressed as a nun enters, carrying a bowl of hot soup. The nun introduces herself as Syrena. Nearby, Carlos is fast asleep in a crib. Marshall is stunned—she has never seen Carlos sleep so peacefully.

Even more surprising is that Syrena seems completely unfazed by Marshall's demonic appearance. Before Marshall can ask about it, she notices that her horns are gone and her eyes have returned to normal. Syrena gently reassures her, saying she wasn't afraid of Marshall even when she brought her in with the horns and eyes—meaning the demonic form vanished on its own when Marshall was brought into Syrena's home.

From that day on, Syrena takes Marshall and Carlos under her wing, caring for them without an ounce of hatred or prejudice. For the first time in a long while, Marshall gets to experience a sense of home again, confident that Carlos will grow up safe and loved.</p>

<p>Years pass. Marshall is now 12 years old, and Carlos is 6. They continue to live happily with Syrena, who has become like a mother to them. Marshall's demonic form has never returned, though she still secretly wonders what truly happened to her six years ago.</p>
